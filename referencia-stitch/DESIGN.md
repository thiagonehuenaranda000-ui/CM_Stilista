## Brand & Style

The brand identity targets discerning clients seeking high-end, transformative hair artistry—specializing in biomolecular straightening, precision blonding, bespoke balayage, and intricate structural braiding. The emotional tone evokes haute couture atelier sophistication: intimate, impeccably polished, fiercely modern, and commanding.

The visual direction combines **Editorial Dark Minimalism** with surgical punches of electric magenta and fine-spun champagne gold. High contrast reigns: deep obsidian surfaces anchor the canvas, allowing before-and-after transformations, glossy hair textures, and vibrant fuchsia callouts to electrify the interface without veering into playful or kitsch territory. Precision lines, crisp geometric bounding boxes, and generous breathing room maintain an uncompromising luxury standard.

## Colors

The palette balances pitch-black canvas depth with hyper-vibrant styling accents:

- **Primary (`#F02D7D` - Haute Magenta):** Reserved strictly for primary focal points, luxury booking CTA actions, active states, and radiant highlights.
- **Secondary (`#D4AF37` - Brushed Champagne Gold):** Deployed for master tier badges, certification ribbons, treatment premium indicators, and delicate metallic accents.
- **Tertiary (`#1E1E24` - Smoked Onyx):** Serves as elevated container surfaces, cards, modal backdrops, and interactive hover backgrounds.
- **Neutral (`#0D0D11` - Obsidian Foundation):** The base dark canvas tone, delivering an immersive backdrop that eliminates visual noise and enhances treatment imagery.
- **Text & Borders:** Off-white (`#F8F8FA`) for primary headlines and data, muted slate (`#9E9EA7`) for secondary hair consultation notes, and hairline borders in low-opacity champagne/fuchsia (`rgba(240, 45, 125, 0.2)`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes classic Italian editorial drama with modern technical precision:

- **Headlines (`Bodoni Moda`):** High-contrast vertical stress, sculpted serifs, and commanding elegance. Sets treatment categories (e.g., *Alisado Biomolecular*, *Balayage Signature*) with editorial authority.
- **Body (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Clean, crisp, neutral sans-serif that remains effortlessly legible across dense appointment instructions and care rituals on deep dark backgrounds.
- **Labels & Metrics (`Space Grotesk`):** Modern, monospaced-adjacent grotesque with an architectural presence. Used in uppercase for category kickers, price metrics, session durations, and step tracking.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column responsive fluid grid governs desktop viewports, condensing to 8 columns on tablet devices and 4 columns on mobile handsets.

- **Desktop & Widescreen:** Generous 48px outer margins preserve an haute salon editorial layout. Service showcases and portfolio transformations occupy expansive horizontal cards spanning 4 or 6 columns.
- **Mobile Handheld:** 20px margins maximize edge-to-edge media visibility. Sticky booking bars anchor firmly to the bottom viewport.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Follows an 8px base grid. Spacers separate high-impact visual showcases with intentional negative space, avoiding clutter and mirroring the curated calm of a luxury private studio.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth relies on tonal gradation and vibrant luminescence rather than standard heavy drop shadows:

- **Base Layer:** `#0D0D11` matte canvas.
- **Container Layer:** `#14141A` with a razor-thin 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Floating Overlays & Modals:** `#1E1E24` infused with a delicate fuchsia-tinted radial shadow (`0 20px 40px -15px rgba(240, 45, 125, 0.25)`).
- **Luminescent Accentuation:** Interactive elements emit a tight neon aura on hover or focus (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(240, 45, 125, 0.45)`). Glassmorphic backdrops utilize `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` over dark treatment photography.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a refined, tailored aesthetic utilizing soft, restrained corner radii (`0.25rem` / `4px`). 

Buttons, service cards, and image viewports avoid round, bubble-like geometries, preserving sharp, architectural silhouette discipline. Chips and status pills feature modest rounding (`rounded-lg` / `8px`) solely to distinguish interactive tags from primary surface architecture.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Reservar Cita):** Solid `#F02D7D` background, sharp 4px radius, white bold sans typography, glowing magenta perimeter on hover (`0 0 16px rgba(240, 45, 125, 0.5)`).
- **Secondary (Detalles del Servicio):** Transparent background, 1px solid `#D4AF37` champagne border, champagne text, filling with subtle gold tint (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.1)`) on interaction.
- **Ghost Action:** Flat surface, `#9E9EA7` text transforming into `#F02D7D` with an underlined hairline indicator.

### Service & Transformation Cards
- Deep smoked surface (`#16161D`) featuring a dual before/after split viewer.
- Top meta badge anchored via Space Grotesk caps.
- Bottom container highlighted by a 1px top border fading from magenta to transparent.

### Chips & Badges
- **Technique Chips (e.g., "Biomolecular", "Decoloración Cero Daño"):** Outlined with `rgba(240, 45, 125, 0.3)`, dark obsidian base, 11px uppercase label font.
- **VIP & Master Badges:** Solid `#D4AF37` text on `#242014` background with a delicate metallic border.

### Form Inputs & Booking Fields
- Obsidian dark fields (`#121217`) framed with 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
- Active focus shifts the border into vibrant `#F02D7D` with zero delay.
- Floating labels transitions set in clean, uppercase `Space Grotesk`.

### Selection Controls (Radios & Checkboxes)
- Custom square radios and checkboxes featuring a 2px bevel.
- Checked state triggers a luminous `#F02D7D` inner square with a centered micro checkmark in pure white.

### Salon-Specific Specialty Components
- **Interactive Strand Shade Slider:** Seamless tonal picker displaying lifting levels (from 1.0 Negro to 10.0 Rubio Platino).
- **Hair Diagnostics Accordion:** High-contrast minimal drawer showing treatment composition, duration, maintenance interval, and post-treatment home care kit.
